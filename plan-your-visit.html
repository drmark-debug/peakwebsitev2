Plan Your Visit

Everything you need to know before visiting Peak by MD, from getting here to understanding our payment options.

Our practice

Personalized Care, Without the Traditional Insurance Model

Peak by MD is a physician-led, cash-based medical practice specializing in longevity, wellness, and aesthetic medicine.

We do not participate directly with insurance plans for office visits. This model allows us to focus on personalized evaluations, individualized treatment plans, and ongoing support.

Membership is not required to receive care.

Insurance & payment

Insurance & Payment Options

HSA & FSA

We accept HSA and FSA payments for qualifying medical services. Eligibility depends on your plan and the type of treatment.

Superbills

Itemized superbills are available upon request for eligible medical services and may be submitted to insurance for potential out-of-network reimbursement. Reimbursement is not guaranteed.

Laboratory Testing

Certain laboratory tests may qualify for insurance coverage. Specialty testing may require out-of-pocket payment.

Flexible Financing

Financing options are available through Cherry and CareCredit, subject to approval and applicable terms.
