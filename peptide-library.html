How we approach peptide safety

At Peak By MD, we believe that promising science should be approached with curiosity, clinical judgment, and transparency.

Before recommending a therapy, we review your medical history, medications, treatment goals, and relevant health markers. When appropriate, we use laboratory testing and objective assessments to better understand your baseline health.

Not all peptides have the same level of clinical evidence. Some medications are supported by extensive human research, while others remain experimental with limited information about long-term safety. Careful supervision helps us recognize problems early, but it does not remove the uncertainty that comes with investigational therapies.

Our goal is to help you understand the potential benefits, limitations, and risks so that treatment decisions are informed and individualized.
