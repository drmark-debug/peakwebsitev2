Sample treatment plan

Daniel's plan, at a glance

Male, 44. Runs three times a week. Annual physical: "normal."

1. What he felt, and what we found

  • "I crash every afternoon, even after a good night's sleep."
    Fasting insulin
    14 µIU/mLEarly resistance
  • "I run three times a week and still can't lose the belly."
    Visceral fat
    2.1 lbElevated
  • "My runs have plateaued and stairs leave me winded."
    VO₂ max
    38 ml/kg/minBelow age average
  • "My dad had a heart attack at 58. I feel fine."
    ApoB
    118 mg/dLAbove optimal

ApoB causes no symptoms. That is why we test for it.

2. Priorities

  1. Steady energy by restoring insulin sensitivity
  2. Lose visceral fat while building lean mass
  3. Raise VO₂ max and lower ApoB to protect his heart

3. The plan

  • ✓
    NutritionProtein-first daily target with a fiber goal, built around his work schedule.
  • ✓
    TrainingTwo strength sessions, weekly Zone 2 work and one interval session.
  • ✓
    Sleep and recoveryConsistent sleep window, tracked with his wearable.
  • ✓
    Medical therapyPhysician-managed lipid-lowering therapy.

4. Retest schedule

Month 3Lipids, insulin and glucose. Adjust therapy.
Month 6Full labs, VO₂ max and 3D body scan.
No more afternoon crashes, a smaller waistline and faster runs. Fasting insulin 14 → 6, visceral fat 2.1 → 1.2 lb, VO₂ max 38 → 44, ApoB 118 → 76.
