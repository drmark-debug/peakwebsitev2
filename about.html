Founder and Medical Director

Dr. Mark Dukshtein, MD

A board-certified internist known for a thoughtful, data-driven approach that integrates conventional medicine, functional diagnostics and modern longevity science.

With a strong background in hospital medicine, Dr. Dukshtein brings extensive experience in complex medical decision-making, risk stratification and evidence-based care. He applies that foundation to preventive medicine, with a focus on metabolic health, hormone balance, body composition, cognitive performance and long-term disease prevention.

His care model starts with precision testing (metabolic analysis, body composition imaging, comprehensive biomarker panels and functional assessments). Those results guide individualized plans that may include nutrition, hormone therapy, peptides, IV therapy and regenerative treatments. He leads with education and steady progression, not quick fixes.
